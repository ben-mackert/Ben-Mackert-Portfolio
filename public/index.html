ECEN 3730 · PCB Design · Fall 2026

Switching-noise demonstrator: good layout vs. bad layout

A 3.5 × 2.5 in two-layer board with the same SN74AHC14 circuit on it twice. One copy has a solid ground plane and decoupling 4 mm from VCC. The other returns ground through a 35 mm, 6 mil trace and has its cap 28 mm away. That makes the board a controlled experiment where layout is the only variable.

The detail I like most: spare gates tied permanently HIGH and LOW act as probes of the chip's on-die VCC and ground, so you can see bounce inside the package for $0.

  • ~60 vs ~600 mVestimated L·dI/dt
  • 0DRC violations
  • 11labeled 10× probe test points
  • Altium
  • Signal integrity
  • LDO stability
  • Hand assembly

ECEN 3753 · Real-Time Operating Systems · Spring 2026

Labyrinth: a gyro-controlled maze game on FreeRTOS

Tilt the STM32F429 board to roll a "drone" through a randomly generated maze. Four FreeRTOS tasks share the work: a 100 Hz gyro integrator, a 50 Hz fixed-point physics engine, an incremental LCD renderer and a software-PWM LED task. They communicate through a message queue, a mutex and event flags. The maze is seeded from gyro thermal noise.

Two notable bugs: integer truncation made the physics silently freeze at small tilt angles (fixed by storing positions in micrometers), and a CMSIS-RTOS2 error code was being read as a valid button flag.

  • 19 / 19functional tests pass
  • 4RTOS tasks, verified in SystemView
  • 2.9%flash used
  • FreeRTOS
  • CMSIS-RTOS2
  • STM32F429
  • SPI gyro
  • SEGGER SystemView

ECEN 4610 · Senior Capstone · Fall 2026 · in progress

SPICE: photon-statistics timing firmware

This is the firmware for a Hanbury Brown–Twiss setup. It timestamps every pulse from two single-photon detectors on one shared clock and builds the g²(τ) histogram that tells you whether light is random, bunched or made of single photons. I run the STM32H723 at 550 MHz with dual-channel timer input capture streamed into circular DMA buffers.

  • 3.6 nstimestamp resolution
  • 101 × 10 nshistogram bins
  • 64-bitwrap-extended time base
